Privacy

This site belongs to Svitlana Biletska, a front-end developer based in Piekary Śląskie, Poland. Questions about this page or your data: hello@svitlanabiletska.com.

Contact form

If you send the form, I receive what you type in it: your name, email, your site (optional), what you need (optional), your message, and which page of this site you sent it from. The form is handled by Netlify Forms, part of Netlify, which hosts this site. I use it only to reply to you. If you want your message deleted, email me.

Analytics, only if you click Accept

If you click Accept, the site loads Google Analytics 4. It shows me which pages people visit and which buttons and links they click (for example the contact buttons, LinkedIn, Upwork and my CV). Google Analytics sets cookies whose names start with _ga and collects technical data such as your device, browser and approximate location. Advertising features are switched off. If you click Decline, or don't choose, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Your choice

It is saved in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie, so the banner doesn't show up again. You can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. If you decline after accepting, the site stops analytics and removes the _ga cookies.

Hosting

Like any web host, Netlify processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages. Fonts and scripts are served from this site; the only third-party service, apart from Netlify, is Google Analytics, and only if you accept.

Links to other sites

LinkedIn, Upwork and Instagram have their own privacy policies; they apply once you open those links.
