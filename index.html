About

Why I do this

I've spent six years building and fixing e-commerce storefronts — mostly Magento and Hyvä, with PrestaShop and WordPress along the way. Somewhere in that time, I started noticing the same pattern on almost every store: small, fixable UX problems that quietly cost sales, because nobody's looking closely enough to catch them.

So I started offering that close look as its own service — a fast, specific audit, then the fix. And because a site that works still needs to look like it's worth trusting, I'm building out the other half of this: visual content for the same small stores I work with.

Process

How a project runs

Four steps, whether it's a quick fix or a larger task — just faster for the small ones.

01

Discovery

You tell me what's wrong, or I point it out first — either way, we agree what "fixed" looks like.

02

Audit & proposal

A short written breakdown of the problem and the fix, with a price and timeline — before anything starts.

03

Build

I work in your environment or a staging copy, with progress updates — no radio silence until "done".

04

Handover

You review it live, I make final tweaks, and you get a short note on what changed and why.
