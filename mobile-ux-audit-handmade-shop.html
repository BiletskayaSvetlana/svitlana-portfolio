Mobile UX audit for a small handmade-goods shop

  • UX audit
  • WooCommerce

01

Context

A small independent WooCommerce shop selling fragile handmade items, mostly as gifts. The owner asked what keeps mobile visitors from buying. I reviewed the public pages only: home, category, product page and cart. I tested on an iPhone and checked speed with PageSpeed Insights.

02

What I found

Six areas, each with the priority it got in the audit.

  1. Priority: High

    Shipping and returns were scattered. The information existed, but in three places, and none of it sat next to the price or the “Add to cart” button. For a fragile gift, that’s exactly where a buyer looks.

  2. Priority: High

    The home page was slow on mobile. The main content took almost 12 seconds to appear in lab tests. Likely causes: a slider that loads extra scripts, plugin files loaded on every page, and oversized photos.

  3. Priority: Medium

    The category page showed one product per screen, and the first product, which was out of stock, was marked only by an icon, with the explanation hidden behind a long press.

  4. Priority: Medium

    On the product page the price and the buy button were far from the top. A large title banner pushed the photo down, and the button came after a long description.

  5. Priority: Medium

    Mixed languages in the cart and product page: some theme texts were left in English, which makes the page look unfinished right before payment.

  6. Priority: Low

    Small, low-contrast details: a tiny remove button in the cart, a nearly invisible reviews tab showing “0”, and a back-to-top button covering text.

Status

Audit delivered; implementation is up to the owner. This is a review of public pages and lab tests, not a before-and-after result, and it doesn’t replace analysis of real sales data.
